Guide 02

Classroom Management

Calm classrooms are built, not found. Clear routines, warm relationships and consistent responses do most of the work before problems start.

Lesson card
Reading time5 minutes
Steps5
Key ideaTeach routines like content: explain, practise, praise.
Step 02.1

Routines first

Decide exactly how students enter, start work, hand things out, ask for help and leave. Teach each routine explicitly, practise it and praise it until it is automatic.

Step 02.2

Relationships matter

Learn names quickly, greet students at the door and notice effort. Students work harder for teachers they feel respected by.

Step 02.3

Clear expectations

Keep a few positively phrased expectations, such as “listen when someone is speaking”, and refer to them often. Model what they look like.

Step 02.4

Respond calmly and consistently

Use the least intrusive response first: a look, moving closer, a quiet word. Stay calm and follow through every time; consistency matters more than severity.

Step 02.5

Reset and repair

After a difficult moment, have a short, private conversation, agree a way forward and start fresh next lesson. A good relationship survives a bad day.
